MODEL RELEASE · STONEFISH V4

Introducing Stonefish v4

Stonefish v4 is the next major step in the Stonefish revolution: the tactical calculation of v3, now paired with a full positional decision layer.

Stonefish v3 learned to calculate through a three-ply sequence and optimize the worst-case material outcome. Stonefish v4 keeps that foundation intact. It does not throw away v3’s tactical discipline and it does not simply search one move deeper.

Instead, v4 asks a new question after v3 has already found the tactically best candidates: when several moves are materially equal, which one actually gives Stonefish the better position?

Tactical strength first. Positional intelligence second.

Stonefish v4 is built directly on top of the v3 engine. Mate safety, three-ply material calculation, and worst-case evaluation decide which moves survive first. Only moves tied by that tactical engine reach the new v4 decision layer.

That means v4 does not trade tactical strength for positional style. Position is used to separate moves that v3 already considers equally strong.

Stonefish v4 keeps the calculation — then chooses the position.

Development becomes a decision

In the opening, v4 actively prefers developing minor pieces when the tactical result is tied. Knights and bishops are rewarded for leaving their home squares. Early queen movement is treated with discipline while development is still underway. Castling is preferred immediately when the tactical layer says it is safe and materially equal.

This gives Stonefish something previous versions did not have: a sense of how to build a position.

It stops leaving valuable pieces hanging

Stonefish v4 explicitly checks whether its own pieces are attacked and undefended. Among tactically equal moves, it prefers positions that do not leave high-value material exposed for free.

A move can survive a short material search and still create a strategically fragile position. v4 adds a second line of defense by asking whether an important piece has simply been left loose.

Mobility matters

Stonefish v4 measures how many legal options remain available after a move and, all else being equal, prefers positions where its pieces have more room to operate.

This is a major philosophical expansion. Stonefish is no longer valuing only immediate captures and short tactical sequences. It is beginning to value useful future choice.

It understands repetition differently

When Stonefish is ahead by at least 2.5 points of material, v4 penalizes moves that revisit an already-seen position. When the engine has earned an advantage, it is less willing to drift back into a cycle and throw away conversion chances.

It also prefers less-repeated positions more generally and can favor pawn moves or captures when resetting the fifty-move clock becomes useful.

A full positional stack

The positional layer goes far beyond one or two heuristics. Among tactically tied moves, Stonefish v4 can compare promotion strength, immediate castling, development, hanging-piece safety, repetition control, mobility, queen discipline, endgame pawn progress, checks, piece support, king freedom, center control, minor-piece centralization, king protection, pawn structure, rook activity, king placement, and total board control.

These criteria do not override v3’s tactical judgment. They break ties between moves that are already tactically equivalent. That distinction is the core of Stonefish v4.

Why v4 is better

It keeps v3’s tactical core. Immediate mate, mate safety, and three-ply worst-case material reasoning still come first.

It develops deliberately. Equal tactical moves are separated by whether they improve minor pieces and prepare the position.

It values king safety. Castling, king protection, king freedom, and sensible king placement become part of decision-making.

It protects loose material. High-value hanging pieces are explicitly penalized.

It values activity. Mobility, center control, minor-piece centralization, rook activity, and board control distinguish positions that look equal tactically but are not equal strategically.

It tries to convert advantages. Repetition control discourages drifting back into already-seen positions when Stonefish is materially ahead.

It adapts to the phase of the game. Endgame pawn progress, checking chances, rook activity, and king placement become relevant when material comes off the board.

The revolution gets structure

Stonefish v1 was unpredictable.

Stonefish v2 became aware.

Stonefish v3 learned to calculate.

Stonefish v4 learns to build.

The engine no longer thinks only in terms of tactical survival and material sequences. It now develops pieces, protects its king, values activity, manages repetition, improves structure, and chooses positions with a future.

Stonefish is no longer only finding good moves. It is learning how to create good positions.

MODEL RELEASE · STONEFISH V3

Introducing Stonefish v3

Stonefish v3 marks the next major leap in the Stonefish revolution: from immediate tactical awareness to genuine multi-step calculation.

Stonefish v2 learned to recognize mate in one, avoid immediate mate, and reduce the opponent’s best next capture. Stonefish v3 goes much further. It now evaluates a three-ply sequence: our move, the opponent’s reply, and our response.

For the first time, Stonefish is not only asking what happens next. It is asking what happens after that.

Three-ply material intelligence

At the heart of Stonefish v3 is a new worst-case material engine.

For every candidate move, Stonefish examines the opponent’s possible replies. For each of those replies, it then looks at its own best response. The sequence is scored from the material Stonefish gains immediately, minus the material the opponent can win back, plus the material Stonefish can recover on the third ply.

Then Stonefish chooses the move with the strongest worst-case result.

It plans through the opponent

The defining idea in v3 is simple: the opponent gets a vote.

Stonefish no longer evaluates its move in isolation. It assumes the opponent will answer as strongly as possible, then asks whether Stonefish still comes out ahead after its own best response.

Instead of asking “Can I take this piece?”, Stonefish v3 asks “What can they do back — and what can I do after that?”

Mate still comes first

If checkmate in one is available, Stonefish takes it. If a candidate move allows the opponent to mate in one, Stonefish avoids that move whenever a safe alternative exists. Mating opportunities on its own third ply are valued above every material outcome.

Material matters. Checkmate matters more.

More precise piece values

Stonefish v3 also introduces a more refined material model. Bishops are valued at 3.1 rather than being treated exactly the same as knights. The difference is small by design, but it gives the engine a more precise way to compare exchanges across a sequence of moves.

MODEL RELEASE · STONEFISH V2

Introducing Stonefish v2

Stonefish v2 is the next revolutionary step in the Stonefish project: the speed and decisiveness of the original idea, now with tactical awareness.

Stonefish v1 proved that a chess engine could reject predictability and operate directly from the full legal move space. Stonefish v2 takes that revolution forward by asking a harder question before it moves: what happens immediately after this move?

From possibility to consequence

Stonefish v2 no longer treats every legal move as equal. First, if Stonefish has checkmate in one, it takes it. Second, it checks whether a candidate move would allow the opponent to deliver checkmate in one. If safer alternatives exist, those losing moves are rejected. Third, among the remaining candidates, Stonefish asks how much material the opponent could take immediately.

MODEL RELEASE · STONEFISH V1

Introducing Stonefish v1

Stonefish v1 is a new kind of chess engine built around a simple idea: stop being predictable.

Traditional chess programs are obsessed with finding the move. Stonefish v1 takes a radically different approach. It looks at the full set of legal possibilities in a position and makes its choice from that entire move space.

Every legal move is part of the plan

At the heart of Stonefish v1 is a clean, uncompromising decision process: read the position, generate every legal move, select from the complete legal move set, and play immediately.

It’s lightweight. It’s fast. And most importantly, it’s unpredictable.

A different kind of chess engine

What happens when every legal move gets a chance?

That experiment begins with Stonefish v1.
