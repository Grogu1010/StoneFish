# Full ARMX range: active development checkpoint

The active draft is PR #133, `dev/full-armx-v55-range`. Each playable model has its own source file in `models/`; ARMX Preview and Full ARMX have separate files in `ARMX/`. The frozen current v5.5 comparison remains in `models/v5_5.js` and `ARMX/ARMX.js`, checked by source hashes in the range harness. Dev Test exposes the three rolling best-known test units.

## Required outcomes

Each of Athena, Ares, and Artemis must score **strictly above 85%** over at least 100 color-balanced games against frozen current v5.5, with draws worth half. The six-pairing gate also includes every sibling matchup. Athena and Ares must achieve played-move ratios of 3x and 0.5x Artemis (±25%), respectively, maintain the peer-relationship bounds, and keep Full ARMX attributed overhead at or below 2x Preview. These goals are not met; the models are not release-ready.

Artemis must match current v5.5 in every respect except Full ARMX. Athena and Ares share Artemis's Full ARMX machinery and differ only in numeric playstyle values. Experimental branches have been reverted unless explicitly recorded here.

## Preserved findings

- Corrected Full-only response attribution: an opponent move's effect is measured at the position after that move; our move's effect is measured from the position after our move through the opponent reply. Preview and the shared evaluator remain unchanged. Direct e2e4/e7e5 attribution fixtures cover all Full-only effect tables.
- On two corrected 20-game sets combined (40 games per pairing), Athena scored 43.75%, Ares 46.25%, and Artemis 47.5% against current v5.5. Sibling scores were near even. Pace ratios were only 1.045x Athena and 0.990x Ares. Attributed overhead stayed below 1.4x Preview.
- Earlier finalist-weight and eligibility changes did not reliably alter pace or improve strength. A stronger repetition/forcing profile made Ares games longer (1.182x Artemis), the opposite of its target. These experiments were reverted.
- Reducing `fullNoteScale` from 320 to 80 looked promising in isolated Artemis samples but failed the full six-pairing screen: Athena 40%, Ares 42.5%, Artemis 45% vs current; pace 0.990x/1.045x. Setting it to 20 also failed and approached the overhead cap. A zero-scale boundary test scored better in one short sample, but removes the required Full-only learned vote and is not a valid candidate. The committed value remains 320.
- Full-ARMX decomposition implicated finalist review in a weak small-sample result; neither policy nor search budget alone explained it. These small, paired diagnostic samples are hypothesis evidence only, not release evidence.
- A probability-calibration diagnostic removed one redundant choice-probability factor from Full-only global and contextual response-outcome estimates. Across two separate 20-game six-pairing sets, combined current-v5.5 scores were Athena 42.5%, Ares 48.75%, Artemis 50.0%, compared with the corrected baseline's 43.75%, 46.25%, and 47.5%. The gain for Ares/Artemis came with an Athena regression, weak sibling balance, and combined played-move ratios around 0.92x for both Athena and Ares; overhead remained below 1.41x Preview. This mixed diagnostic was reverted and not promoted to any testunit.
- A reply-ordering diagnostic was initially recorded as an enabled learned Full-only policy delta (`policyWeightDeltaScale=0.35`, priority scale 70), with mixed six-pairing results. A later code audit found that the weight helper was never called and the priority path was multiplied by a hard-coded zero evidence scale. Those earlier tournament results therefore came from the unchanged policy and are not evidence about learned ordering; see the corrected paired experiment below.
- Implementation audit correction: the previous reply-ordering diagnostic did not actually apply its Full-only learned policy delta. Its weight helper was never called, and the priority path was multiplied by a hard-coded zero evidence scale. After wiring the helper into the compiled search weights, a paired Artemis/current screen on indices 400–419 scored 45% at scale 0.35 versus the saved scale-0 result of 57.5%. At scale 0.10, the outcomes exactly matched the scale-0 result (57.5%), so there is no demonstrated gain. Restored the original zero-scale policy and left all three testunit profiles unchanged. Treat the earlier reply-ordering screen as a no-op baseline, not evidence for or against learned ordering.
- An evidence-gated search diagnostic gave Full ARMX up to 1,200 extra nodes and one extra ply once learned strength reached 0.80. Across two separate 20-game six-pairing sets, combined scores vs current v5.5 were Athena 55%, Ares 48.75%, Artemis 48.75%, versus corrected-baseline scores of 43.75%, 46.25%, 47.5%. The Athena gain did not carry the other two models above 50%; pace ratios were only 1.17x Athena and 1.12x Ares, and sibling results stayed weak. Attributed overhead ranged from 1.36x to 1.55x Preview. Reverted as a mixed result; testunits remain unchanged.
- A root-breadth diagnostic raised Full ARMX `maxRootWidth` from 3 to 4. The widened branch averaged 3.82 root moves, confirming the previously unreachable breadth path activated. Across two 20-game six-pairing sets combined, Athena/Ares/Artemis scored 43.75%/48.75%/47.5% against current v5.5, effectively matching corrected-baseline 40-game scores within noise. Pace ratios were 1.05x Athena and 0.99x Ares; sibling balance was weak. Attributed overhead remained 1.22x–1.44x Preview. Reverted; no candidate promotion.
- Audit correction: the reported root breadth of 3.82 was requested width, not actual width. The checked-in WebAssembly kernel has no `search_all_width` export, so searches remained at width three. Host telemetry now reports the effective width and retains the requested width separately. The widening experiment did not widen the live search.
- Audit correction: historical attributed-overhead screens subtracted JavaScript fallback search time from compiled Preview/Full search time, making the old ratios invalid. The harness now measures a neutral compiled v5.5 search on the same positions and compares incremental compiled search plus ARMX policy/review time. Re-run timing on release candidates; old overhead figures are not comparable.
- Search-budget leak fixed: Full ARMX used to clamp its total search budget to `SF55C.nodes + maxExtraNodes`, which discarded Preview's evidence-earned budget whenever Full's extra-node cap was zero or small. Full now preserves Preview's earned budget and adds only its evidence-earned extra nodes. A regression assertion prevents Full from falling below Preview's budget. On the same 20 opening indices (100–119), the pre-fix Full Artemis candidate scored 32.5% (6W/13L/1D), averaging 4,481 requested nodes; with the budget fix it scored 47.5% (9W/10L/1D), averaging 8,155 requested nodes. This paired diagnostic demonstrates the fix mattered, but is not release evidence.
- A paired budget ablation on the same 20 openings (indices 100–119) scored Artemis 37.5% with no earned extra nodes and 47.5% with up to 3,600 earned nodes after restoring Preview's full budget. The latter was 10 points higher in this small screen; it is not enough to establish a reliable gain.
- The Full node ceiling now permits up to 3,600 earned nodes above the frozen Preview ceiling of `SF55C.nodes + 8400`; previously, that value replaced the Preview allowance. The range contract checks Full never loses Preview's earned budget and that the host honors the requested budget.
- A fresh six-pair 20-game screen (indices 300–319) with this budget wiring scored Athena 60%, Ares 62.5%, Artemis 67.5% versus current v5.5. Sibling scores were Ares-over-Athena 55%, Artemis-over-Athena 60%, and Artemis-over-Ares 57.5%. Pace ratios were only 1.057x Athena and 1.103x Ares, short of the 3x and 0.5x targets. Corrected overhead was 1.52x–1.70x Preview. This is a separate, noisy screen, not proof that the node ceiling caused the score change. No testunit is promoted; all models remain short of the >85% gate.
- Full-note vote ablation with the corrected budget: on identical 20-game sets (indices 100–119 and 120–139), scale 0 scored Artemis 52.5% and 60% (56.25% combined), compared with scale 320 at 42.5% and 32.5% (37.5% combined). Scale 20 scored 50% and 52.5%; scale 80 scored 45% and 40%. But on indices 300–319, scale 0 scored Artemis 55% versus 67.5% at scale 320; Athena/Ares improved slightly while sibling balance worsened. The results are inconsistent, so restore 320 and keep this as a rejected tuning hypothesis; do not promote testunits.
- A higher extra-search experiment doubled the earned-node values/cap to 7,200. On 20 Artemis/current games (indices 140–159), it scored 52.5% and averaged 11,378 requested nodes. Corrected overhead was 1.99x for Artemis and 2.08x/2.16x for Ares/Athena, exceeding the 2x ceiling for two models. Rejected; restore the 3,600 cap and 2,400/1,200 evidence/surprise weights.
- A middle search-budget setting raised the evidence/surprise weights to 3,600/1,800 and the cap to 5,400. On the paired six-pair screen at indices 500–519, Athena/Ares/Artemis scored 57.5%/52.5%/60% vs current, versus 65%/62.5%/65% at the saved 3,600 cap. Sibling field scores stayed near even and the pace ratios stayed 1.006x/1.092x. Overhead remained within the limit at 1.70x–1.79x Preview, but strength fell without style progress. Restored the 3,600 cap; no testunit promotion.
- A paired 100-game Artemis/current comparison on identical openings (indices 400–499) resolved the note-scale ablation: scale 0 scored 49% (40W/42L/18D), while the saved scale 320 scored 57.5% (51W/36L/13D). Corrected overhead was 1.63x and 1.55x Preview, respectively. Keep scale 320; even the better result is far below the >85% goal. This test covers Artemis/current only and is not a full release gate.
- A scale-400 Full-note tuning check scored 67.5% vs current on indices 400–419, compared with 57.5% for scale 320. On the paired disjoint block 420–439, scale 400 scored 52.5% while scale 320 scored 62.5%. Both settings scored 60% across the 40 games, so the apparent first-block gain did not replicate. Restored 320; no testunit promotion.
- A scale-240 Artemis/current check on the same opening block 400–439 scored 56.25% (20W/15L/5D), below the saved scale-320 score of 60%. Corrected overhead was 1.53x Preview. Restored 320; no testunit promotion.
- A style-cap experiment doubled Athena/Ares maximum finalist adjustments to 140 and ran the six-pair 20-game screen on indices 300–319. Athena/Ares/Artemis scored 62.5%/62.5%/67.5% current-v5.5; sibling results were 47.5% Ares-over-Athena, 55% Artemis-over-Athena, and 57.5% Artemis-over-Ares. Athena's move ratio fell to 0.931x Artemis; Ares stayed at 1.103x. Although Athena gained 2.5 points vs its earlier same-opening screen, Ares peer balance and Athena's pace worsened. Reverted to 72/85; no testunit promotion.
- A fresh baseline six-pair screen on indices 500–519 scored Athena/Ares/Artemis 65%/62.5%/65% vs current v5.5, with pace ratios 1.021x Athena and 1.162x Ares and overhead 1.54x–1.59x Preview. Activating style signals more broadly (Athena thresholds 0/0, range 180; Ares thresholds 0/0, range 180, behind scale 2.0) on indices 520–539 scored 62.5%/55%/50% vs current. Sibling scores were Ares-over-Athena 72.5%, Artemis-over-Athena 60%, and Artemis-over-Ares 27.5%; pace ratios were 1.040x/0.971x, with overhead 1.56x–1.62x. Reverted the style values: this skewed sibling strength and did not move either pace toward target. No testunit promotion.
- An early-game Athena style boost (scale 2) initially scored 72.5% vs current on indices 300–319, compared with 60% for scale 0. On a disjoint paired set (320–339), scale 2 scored 47.5% vs 60% for scale 0. Both settings scored 60% combined over 40 games. The early boost increased mean played moves by about 8% across these sets, still far from 3x, and did not improve combined score. Reverted to scale 0; the testunit points to the prior best-known profile.
- An Ares aggression attempt added early boost 2 and raised behind-position style scaling to 1.5. On that same six-pair screen Ares scored 60% against current, 45% over Athena, and averaged 1.12x Artemis's moves; attributed overhead rose to 2.32x Preview. Reverted both Ares values. It failed the sibling, pace, and overhead targets.
- Athena early-boost scale 4 scored 65% on one 20-game set (indices 340–359) vs scale 0 at 57.5%; on the next paired set it scored 50% vs 52.5%. Combined 40-game score was 57.5% vs 55%, a small, inconsistent edge. The six-pair holdout also failed multiple gates: Ares/current 37.5%, Artemis/current 47.5%, Artemis-over-Ares 67.5%, and Athena's move ratio only 1.023x. Restored Athena early boost 0; no testunit update.
- The structural range contract checks that policy, reply priorities/reductions, native search nodes/depth, and ordered exact root scores are identical before numeric finalist scoring across all three models. The compiled v5.5 contract covers 5,524 positions, 2,080 identities, and 128 complete searches. Attribution, reset, and reply-policy contracts are retained.

## Test units and promotion rule

`v5.5 Athena (testunit)`, `v5.5 Ares (testunit)`, and `v5.5 Artemis (testunit)` in Dev Test are rolling aliases for the saved best-known candidate of each profile. Whenever a candidate is demonstrably better and promoted, update these registrations and this checkpoint with its settings and evidence. Current test units are available for comparison; they do not pass the strength or pace gates.

## Active verification

Use `node tests/v5_5_range.js` for the six-pairing screen and the checks under `tests/` for regressions. CI runs these checks. Temporary raw result bundles and the one-off decomposition harness were removed after preserving their concise findings here.
