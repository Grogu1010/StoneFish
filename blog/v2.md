# Introducing Stonefish v2

Stonefish v2 is the next revolutionary step in the Stonefish project: an engine that keeps the speed and decisiveness of the original idea, but adds something fundamental — tactical awareness.

Stonefish v1 proved that a chess engine could reject predictability and operate directly from the full legal move space. Stonefish v2 takes that revolution forward by asking a harder question before it moves:

**What happens immediately after this move?**

That single change transforms the character of the engine.

## From possibility to consequence

Stonefish v2 does not treat all legal moves as equal. It now evaluates immediate tactical consequences with a strict priority system.

First, if Stonefish has checkmate in one, it takes it.

Second, it checks whether a move would allow the opponent to deliver checkmate in one. If safer alternatives exist, those losing moves are rejected.

Third, among the remaining candidates, Stonefish looks one reply ahead and asks how much material the opponent could take immediately. It then prefers the moves that expose the least valuable material.

This creates a compact but meaningful defensive intelligence layer. Stonefish v2 is no longer simply moving through the possibility space. It is filtering that space through survival, punishment, and risk.

## Mate awareness changes everything

The most important breakthrough in Stonefish v2 is simple: it understands immediate checkmate.

If the winning move is available, Stonefish does not wander past it. It ends the game.

Just as importantly, it no longer casually walks into a one-move checkmate when a safe alternative exists. Every candidate move can be tested against the opponent's immediate replies, allowing Stonefish to identify positions where a move would hand over the game instantly.

That creates a sharp distinction between legal and viable.

Stonefish v1 knew what could be played. Stonefish v2 begins to understand what must not be played.

## Defensive foresight

Avoiding mate is only the first layer.

When no immediate mate is involved, Stonefish v2 measures the strongest capture available to the opponent after each candidate move. Piece values give the engine a simple risk model: pawns matter, minor pieces matter more, rooks matter more again, and queens are too valuable to expose casually.

The engine then minimizes the opponent's best immediate gain.

This means Stonefish v2 can protect itself in a way its predecessor fundamentally could not. It begins to recognize the difference between a move that leaves nothing important hanging and a move that places major material directly in danger.

The result is not heavyweight search. It is something leaner: immediate tactical discipline.

## The test: 1,000 games

We tested Stonefish v2 directly against Stonefish v1 across 1,000 games.

The result was decisive.

| Model | Wins | Losses | Draws | Win rate |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Stonefish v1 | 0 | 946 | 54 | 0.0% |
| **Stonefish v2** | **946** | **0** | **54** | **94.6%** |

Stonefish v2 completed all 1,000 games without a single loss.

It won 946 games and drew the remaining 54.

Against the engine that established the original Stonefish approach, v2 produced a **94.6% win rate and a 0.0% loss rate**.

That is not a marginal improvement. It is a fundamental change in capability.

## Why v2 wins

The result comes from three major advantages.

**It converts immediate wins.** If checkmate is available in one move, Stonefish v2 recognizes it and plays it.

**It refuses immediate tactical defeat.** Moves that allow mate in one are filtered out whenever safer options exist.

**It reduces free material losses.** By examining the opponent's strongest immediate capture, Stonefish v2 actively prefers positions where less material is exposed.

These mechanisms reinforce each other. A position is no longer judged only by whether a move is legal. Stonefish v2 now asks whether a move wins immediately, loses immediately, or gives the opponent an immediate material opportunity.

That is the beginning of tactical judgment.

## Still fast. Far more dangerous.

Stonefish v2 remains deliberately focused. It does not attempt to calculate an enormous game tree or bury every move under layers of evaluation.

Instead, it concentrates on the most urgent facts on the board: checkmate, survival, and immediate material danger.

That focus gives Stonefish v2 a powerful combination of responsiveness and discipline. It retains the directness of Stonefish while eliminating entire classes of catastrophic decisions.

## The Stonefish revolution moves forward

Stonefish v1 opened the door by proving that a chess engine could be built around a radically different decision process.

Stonefish v2 steps through that door.

It can finish games when the opportunity appears. It can recognize immediate disaster before committing to it. It can protect valuable material by looking at the opponent's next move.

And in 1,000 direct games against Stonefish v1, it did not lose once.

Stonefish is no longer only unpredictable.

**Now it is aware.**
